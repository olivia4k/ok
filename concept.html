Architecture is the art of
how we meet the ground.

Interior Architect Portfolio operates at the intersection of space, material, and atmosphere. Founded in Warsaw, the studio works across residential, commercial, and cultural projects—each one a precise response to its context.

We do not pursue a signature style. Instead, we pursue a quality of attention—a slowness that allows the inherent logic of a space to emerge. Materials are chosen for their honesty. Light is considered before furniture. Silence is the beginning of every brief.

03 / Proces

Method & Material

I. Discovery

A period of deep listening. Site visits, archival research, conversations about light and memory. No drawings yet.

II. Concept

A single spatial idea. Translated into sketches, material samples, and light studies before any floor plan is drawn.

III. Resolution

Technical drawings, material specifications, coordination with engineers and craftspeople. Precision at every joint.

IV. Delivery

On-site presence throughout construction. The final act: placing objects, adjusting light, allowing the space to breathe.

04 / In Focus

Penthouse Sopot
— A Study in Restraint

Location Sopot, Poland
Year 2025
Area 380 m²
Materials Limestone · Oak · Plaster
Typology Private Residence

The brief was radical in its simplicity: remove everything until what remains is irreducible. The result is a 380-square-metre apartment on the Baltic coast that breathes with the rhythm of the tides—limestone floors that cool in the morning, oak panels that warm by evening, and a view that makes every interior gesture secondary.
